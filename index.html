Every submitted plan starts in Draft Mode until the Admin approves it. Urgent plans can be fast-tracked by pinging the Admin.

Invite everyone or select specific people only—uninvited members won't see the event on their calendar.

🔒 Auto-Locking FOMO Chat

🚗 Carpool & Seat Matrix

Track whose car or bike is going, available seats, pickup timings, and Google Maps pins.

💸 Smart Expense Split & Exclusions

Log group bills, auto-deduct advance payments, exclude specific people from specific items, and see who pays whom.
