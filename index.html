How SceneOn – The Pakka Plan Works

1. 🔑 6-Digit Personal Identity

Register once with your Name & Mobile Number to get a unique 6-digit code synced to the cloud. Forgot it? Recover it anytime using your phone number.

2. ⏳ Admin Approval Gate

Every submitted plan stays in Draft Mode until the Admin approves it. Urgent plans can be fast-tracked by pinging the Admin.

3. 🚫 No Easy Bail Excuse Rule

Selecting "Not Coming" isn't counted until you provide a valid reason. Plus, bailing out locks you out of that event's live chat!

4. 🚗 Carpool & Expense Splits

Coordinate whose car everyone is taking, collect advance fees, log trip bills, and let Admin exclude specific members from specific expenses.
